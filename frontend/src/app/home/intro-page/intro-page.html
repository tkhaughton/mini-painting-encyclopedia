A Guide For First Time Painters

My philosophy in all creative pursuits is to first and foremost pursue what excites and interests you. However, if you're just getting into the hobby and find yourself paralyzed by uncertainty or indecision, or just want to review the basics of how one paints miniatures at all, here are some recommendations on places to start.

First, learn how to apply paint to a miniature in the first place. Look at paintbrushes to familiarize yourself with how your primary tool works (something too many experienced painters neglect). Review paint thinning, to understand how to work with miniature paint, and why everyone is yelling at you to thin your paints. Finally review brush maintenance to learn some best practices for taking care of your brush and making sure it stays in good condition when you're done painting. It can also be helpful to review primer to understand how to properly prepare a model for painting, but if you're just getting started, painting directly onto the plastic is good enough to get started with.

To learn more about paint, review the three fundamentals of color - chroma, value, and hue. Developing an eye for these things is a critical foundational skill for any painter. When you feel comfortable with those, learning about value contrast is an excellent next step.

Finally, if you`d like to familiarize yourself with some of the common beginner tools and recommendations in the hobby, check out the pages for zenithal highlighting, washes, and contrast_paint.

Those fundamentals alone will get you quite far. Beyond that, I encourage you to click around on the related skills for those entries, or simply search for things you`ve seen that interest you and review prerequisites. Once you have the basics, let your curiosity and interest guide you the rest of the way.
