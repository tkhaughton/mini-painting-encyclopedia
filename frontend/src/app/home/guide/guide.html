How To Use This Encyclopedia

This encyclopedia is designed to serve as a broad, high-level reference for miniature painting. To that end, these are the things the encyclopedia was designed to do:

  1. Define common terms, and situate them both within miniature painting and the broader art world.
  2. Identify conceptual and technical prerequisites to higher-level miniature painting techniques.
  3. Provide references on where to go to learn more about a given topic.

Here are some things the encyclopedia was not designed to do:

  1. Give in depth, step by step explanations and tutorials for painting techniques or tools.
  2. Offer shortcuts or tricks for getting miniatures table-ready quickly.

There is already a rich community of miniature painting content creators with tutorials for anything you could imagine; the goal of this encyclopedia is not to replace them or co-opt their work, but rather to put them in context so you can know what you're looking for when you do go searching for tutorials. To use this encyclopedia most effectively, I would encourage you to search for terms or ideas that interest you, identify any gaps in your knowledge in the prerequisites, and then review the recommended resources for more detailed tutorials.

If you are very new to the hobby, and don`t even know where to begin, I recommend checking out our Where To Start page to get a sense of some fundamental skills that are foundational to the hobby. Frankly, I would recommend this even if you're not new - folks in our hobby have a way of skimming over fundamental ideas and concepts, and reviewing foundational knowledge like the way your brush works can help you improve significantly as a painter.
